The local SDK/RPC run, with synthetic source facts on a local validator, admitted three backed work lots, revoked the initiating actor, established bills of 900, zero and zero fixture units, and paid 900 atomic fixture units to the supplier from financier custody. The buyer retained 900 of principal debt. Company, other-company and personal cash remained unchanged.

The earned lot had a 2,000-unit cap, a supplier claim of 1,400 and retained source facts supporting 900. Finalization established 900, released 1,100 of unused principal and retained 90 of interest authority. Each zero bill released its full cap. A fourth signed admission was refused after revocation and never became a bill.

Distinct transaction signers serviced the accepted rights after the initiating actor lost authority.
