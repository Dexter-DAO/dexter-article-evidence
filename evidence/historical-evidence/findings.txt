disabled. They verify the saved seller binding, document and quote; both agent
voucher signatures and derived claims; retained order/response hashes; both
seller receipt signatures; all five saved copycat decisions; and both signed

transactions. The source includes a case-two fixture and case-three clone
fixture. Each fixture keeps its own identity and scope.

WHAT EACH OFFLINE RESULT MEANS

The seller commitment is checked against the ceremony nonce retained in the
record. The full verifier obtains that nonce independently from the finalized
approval transaction. Receipt signature checks authenticate their signed URL,
payer, network and issuance time. The separate order/response checks establish
agreement among the retained bytes. The full verifier also checks issuance
against the chain's reservation and lock times.

The copied mainnet record contains transaction references and recorded results.
It does not contain all RPC transaction responses. This package build did not
run a fresh full mainnet replay. The exact input, pinned verifier and command
are ready for a reader's own archival RPC.
